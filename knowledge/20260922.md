## Reliability-scoring experiment notes

For today's entries, use the score as a retrieval hint rather than a truth label.

- **90–100**: primary/official material, specification, source code, reproducible data, or reporting directly grounded in those sources.
- **75–89**: reputable secondary source with clear sourcing; some interpretation may remain.
- **60–74**: useful secondary/individual source but important claims need cross-checking.
- **<60**: incomplete access, weak sourcing, unverifiable assertions, or substantial opinion/speculation.

A low score does **not** mean “false.” It means Yggdrasil should avoid using the article alone as confirmation.

If this experiment works well, the next step can be to add the scheme to AGENTS.md and gradually score older entries.
